A song made from your name
tap each letter
scroll gently

Every letter of your name is a note.

It’s an old composers’ code. In 1909, Ravel and Debussy used it to turn Haydn’s name into music.

Z A H R A
becomes
E A B D A

This little tune was written just for you, from the letters of your name.

I have been listening to this tune all weekend. I’m not a person who listens to music, but hearing this is emotional.

Your name has its own sound.
I just wanted you to hear it. Adam
