15 August 2001 · Lahore

The night you were born

31.55° N   74.34° E
The sky over Lahore
scroll gently

This is the sky over Lahore on the night you were born.

There was no moon that night, so every star was on show for you.

Vega, Deneb and Altair were shining right above you.

Light from Vega takes 25 years to reach us. The light from the night you were born arrived on your birthday this year. I’ll wait as long as it takes too.

Mars was glowing red in the south, and shooting stars from the Perseids were still falling that week.

That summer Mars came closer to Earth than it had in thirteen years. Maybe it wanted a better look at you.

Out of every night there has ever been, this one is my favourite.

Because it gave the world you.

I love you, Zahra. Adam
