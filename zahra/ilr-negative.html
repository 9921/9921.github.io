Zahra,

If you’re reading this one, I’m so sorry. I really am. I know this isn’t the news you wanted, and I know how much you were hoping for it. I was hoping for it too, with my whole heart.

Before anything else, I just want you to breathe. You don’t have to be okay right now. You don’t have to be strong, you don’t have to have a plan, and you don’t have to pretend it doesn’t hurt. Cry if you need to cry. Be angry if you need to be angry. All of it is allowed.

I know how much you’ve put into this. Years of it. All the hardship, all the money that went into it, all the paperwork and the waiting. All the days you were annoyed and emotional, and all those late nights when you couldn’t sleep because your mind wouldn’t switch off. I saw how much it took out of you.

So please hear me when I say this: none of that was wasted. Not one bit of it.

This decision is not a judgement on you. It doesn’t say anything about who you are, how hard you’ve worked, or how much you deserve to be here. It’s a decision on a piece of paper, made by people who have never met you. They don’t know you. I do.

And I want you to know, I have never once judged you for your status, and I never will. It has never changed how I see you. Not before, not now, not ever. You are still you. My Zahra.

I know right now it might feel like the end of the road, but it isn’t. It’s a setback, and I know it’s a really painful one, but there are still options and there is still a way forward. You don’t have to figure any of it out tonight. Tonight is just for letting yourself feel it.

When you’re ready, and only when you’re ready, you’ll take the next step. One step at a time. Speak to a solicitor, look at what the options are, and work out the plan. You’ve done hard things before, and you’ll do this too.

Remember who you are. You’re the girl who kept going through years of this, when most people would have given up a long time ago. That girl doesn’t stop here.

Some of the best stories have a chapter where everything looks lost. This is just that chapter. It’s not the ending.

Delayed doesn’t mean denied. A no today is not a no forever. It just means the road is a little longer than we wanted it to be, and you’ve already proven you can walk long roads.

You are one of the strongest people I know. You’ve already been through so much and you’re still standing. This doesn’t change that. If anything, it proves it.

I believe in you, and I truly believe this will work out. Maybe not in the way or on the timeline we hoped, but it will.

You’ve got this, Zahra. I mean it. You’ve got this.

Be gentle with yourself tonight, baby girl. Eat something, drink some water, wrap yourself up and get some rest. Tomorrow is a new day.

All my love, always,
Adam ♡
