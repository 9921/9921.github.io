Zahra,

If you’re reading this, it means you got it. You actually got it! I’ve said that line back to myself so many times and I still can’t stop smiling. I’m so, so happy for you.

Do you even realise what you’ve just done?

Think about how long this has been part of your life. Years of it. Years of waiting, checking your emails, checking your phone, and planning your whole life around dates and decisions that were in someone else’s hands.

All the hardship. All the money that went into it, money you worked so hard for and had to hand over again and again. All the paperwork, all the waiting, all the not knowing.

And all the times it got to you. The days you were annoyed and fed up with the whole thing. The days you were emotional and just needed to let it out. The late nights when you couldn’t switch your brain off, wondering what was going to happen. I remember those nights. I remember how heavy it all felt for you.

And now it’s done. It’s over. You got your ILR.

No more waiting. No more what ifs. No more feeling like your future is on hold.

You worked so hard for this, Zahra. Nobody handed it to you. You fought for it, you stayed strong when it would have been so easy to give up, and you just kept going. You deserve every single bit of this.

I want you to know something. I’ve always been by your side through all of it. The good news, the bad news, the long waits and the stressful days. And not once, not for a single second, have I ever judged you for your status. It never changed how I saw you. To me you were never a case or a visa or a piece of paper. You were always just you. My Zahra.

Even when things between us got hard, I never stopped hoping this day would come for you, and I never stopped cheering for you.

Now you can finally breathe. Properly breathe. You can celebrate without anything hanging over you anymore.

And the world is yours now. Start planning all those trips you’ve been dreaming about. Paris, Rome, Santorini, all around Europe, and a beach somewhere warm. Go wherever you want, whenever you want.

And just one more year until your passport. One more year, and then honestly, there’s no stopping you. I can already picture you holding it.

I know I was only one part of this. Sometimes all I could do was listen, or hold you, or tell you it was all going to be okay. But I’m so grateful I got to be there for it. I’m so glad I got to be part of your journey, and it’s a part of our story I’ll never forget.

Wherever life takes you next, I hope you always remember how strong you were through all of this. You should be so proud of yourself. I know I am.

I’m so proud of you, baby girl. So, so proud.

Now go celebrate. Eat something amazing, put your favourite song on, call everyone you love, and enjoy every single second of this. You’ve waited long enough.

All my love, always,
Adam ♡
