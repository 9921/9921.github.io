Zahra,

If you’re reading this one, it means you’re still waiting. I know. I know how hard this part is.

Honestly, the waiting might be the hardest part of all of it. Checking your phone, checking your emails, your heart jumping every time something comes through, and then… nothing. Again. It’s exhausting, and it’s completely okay to be tired of it.

But I want you to remember something. No news is not bad news. It just means it’s not here yet.

You’ve already done the hard part. You’ve done everything you possibly could. Everything has been sent, everything is in, and now it’s just time. And time is something you’ve already proven you can handle better than anyone I know.

Think about how far you’ve come. All those years, all the hardship, all the money, all the late nights and all the stress. You got through every single bit of it. This last little stretch of waiting is not going to beat you.

And no matter how long this takes, it doesn’t change a single thing about how I see you. It never has, and it never will.

So for now, try to give your mind a little break. You don’t have to sit there refreshing your emails all day. Go out, see your friends, eat something nice, watch something that makes you laugh until your stomach hurts. Let life carry on, because the answer will come whether you’re watching for it or not.

And when it does come, whatever it says, you’ll handle it. You always do.

I have a really good feeling about this. I really do. I can already picture the day you finally get your answer, and that smile on your face when you read it.

Hold on a little longer, baby girl. Be patient with it, and be patient with yourself.

Good things take time. And you deserve the very best things.

You’ve got this.

All my love, always,
Adam ♡
